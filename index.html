What we do

Eight services. One point of contact.

From the first market question to long-term growth, we coordinate what your business needs in Egypt.

Scroll to move across the bridge — each service is one span of your path into Egypt.

01

Market Research & Feasibility Studies

Before any commitment, you need a clear picture of the market. Market intelligence tailored to your sector, and feasibility work that weighs risks and returns.

  • Sector and market intelligence
  • Competitor and customer insights
  • Overview of the regulatory landscape
  • Feasibility study coordination
  • Site selection and comparative analysis
02

Business Establishment & Corporate Services

Setting up involves many steps and many offices. We coordinate the full process so your company is registered, licensed and ready to operate.

  • Company formation and registration
  • Licensing and permits coordination
  • Tax and trade registration support
  • Administrative coordination
  • Operational setup: office, utilities, telecom
03

Strategic Partnerships & Business Development

The right partner is the difference between a slow start and real momentum. We identify and introduce reliable partners aligned with your objectives.

  • Partner, distributor and supplier matchmaking
  • Introductions to potential clients
  • B2B meetings and business missions
  • Support for trade fairs and events
  • Follow-up until cooperation is in place
04

Government Liaison & Investment Facilitation

Clear, consistent communication with public institutions. We help you prepare, submit and follow up — and understand the incentives that may apply.

  • Coordination with relevant authorities
  • Investment procedure support and follow-up
  • Guidance on incentives and tax benefits
  • Information on economic and free zones
  • Investor visa and residency facilitation
05

Real Estate & Industrial Opportunities

Location shapes cost, logistics and growth. We find commercial, industrial and logistics sites that match your operational needs and expansion plans.

  • Office and commercial space
  • Industrial facilities and warehouses
  • Land and industrial park options
  • Site visits and comparison
  • Lease and purchase negotiation support
06

Banking & Financial Relations

A smooth financial setup is essential from day one. Introductions to banks and financial institutions, and coordination of the steps that follow.

  • Banking introductions
  • Account opening coordination
  • Relationships with financial institutions
  • Coordination with financial service providers
  • Ongoing administrative support
07

Legal & Regulatory Coordination

Every investment must stand on solid legal ground. We connect you with qualified legal professionals and coordinate corporate and regulatory matters.

  • Access to qualified law firms
  • Coordination of corporate matters
  • Contract and compliance coordination
  • Labour and employment matters
  • Regulatory follow-up

Legal advice is provided by licensed legal professionals.

08

Ongoing Business Advisory

Our role does not end once you are established. We remain a practical partner as your business grows and expands in Egypt and the region.

  • Business development support
  • Operational coordination
  • Expansion planning
  • Recruitment and workforce coordination
  • Networking with industry peers
